Little Rabbit Metronome - Free Online Web Metronome

Little Rabbit Metronome is a free, no-download online web metronome that works right in your browser. It supports 40 to 208 BPM, covers common time signatures including 4/4, 3/4, 2/4, 6/8, 5/4, and 7/8, and accepts custom signatures. Three sound modes are available: traditional drum, steady tick, and voice count. Great for piano, guitar, drums, dance, singing, running rhythm training, and music education. The tool runs entirely in your local browser — no registration, no ads, no distractions.

Common Uses

  • Piano, guitar, drum practice — provides a stable, accurate tempo
  • Dance, yoga, and running rhythm training — freely adjustable BPM
  • Music education — voice mode is great for children's music introduction
  • Recording and composition — supports multiple time signatures for classical, jazz, pop, rock, and more

Why Choose Little Rabbit Metronome

Completely free, no sign-up, no app to install — just open the page. Includes traditional, steady, and voice count sound modes. Customize BPM and time signature; all settings are saved locally. Works on phones, tablets, and desktops — a portable tool for music and dance practice.
