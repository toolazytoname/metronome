How to use

Open jpq.weichao.studio/en in a browser. Search WeChat for 小兔头节拍器. On iOS / Android the core (play, BPM, meter, default voice counting) stays free.

No sound?

On the web, turn off the iPhone ringer switch. The native apps use a playback audio session so they should still sound when the switch is on silent. Check the volume slider.

Sound Workshop / Restore

The optional one-time SKU studio.weichao.jpq.soundpack unlocks extra timbres only. If the pack disappears after reinstall, tap Restore purchases in Settings. Default voice counting does not require a purchase.

Tips
