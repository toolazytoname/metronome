Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-26 · Contact: lazywc@gmail.com

Bunny Metronome (web, WeChat mini-program, iOS / Android) is a no-account tool. There is no sign-up and no user profile.

Local preferences

Settings live only on your device (web localStorage, mini-program storage, App UserDefaults / DataStore): BPM, time signature, sound mode, volume, language, haptic and keep-awake. Clearing app data resets them. There is no cloud sync.

Optional sound pack

The iOS / Android “Sound Workshop” is a one-time optional product (SKU studio.weichao.jpq.soundpack). Default voice counting and the basic clicks stay free. Purchase and Restore purchases are recorded by Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing. We do not keep a user table and cannot share one receipt across stores.

Network

  • Web / mini-program: anonymous analytics (Umeng, GA4). No name, email, or practice audio.
  • iOS / Android: Firebase Analytics records in-app events (open, play/pause, BPM, meter, sound mode, settings). We use an app-instance ID, not advertising IDs (IDFA / GAID), and not for cross-app ads. Events go to Google so we can see daily activity. IAP still talks only to the system store.
  • No microphone, contacts, or location permission.

Children

The app is suitable for music beginners and does not collect personal data from children under 13. No UGC, no social features.
