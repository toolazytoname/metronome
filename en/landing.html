FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you ask

Is it really free forever? Any ads?

Completely free · forever · no ads · no in-app purchases · no sign-up. The dev runs on optional tips, no paywalls.

Does it work offline?

The web version needs an initial load. The WeChat mini-program version caches everything and works offline. All settings (BPM, time signature, sound mode) save locally.

Will my BPM and time signature be saved?

Yes. Your last BPM, time signature and sound mode auto-restore — your practice flow stays intact.

Works on iPad / tablet? How's the touch experience?

Yes — phone, tablet, desktop all supported. Touch targets are generous so you can tweak BPM one-handed mid-practice.

Can I use custom time signatures like 5/8?

Yes. Presets cover 4/4, 3/4, 2/4, 6/8, 5/4, 7/8 — and the custom field accepts any combination.
